READUO

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Readuo account in the app: Profile → Account & sign-in → Delete account. Confirm the warning and sign in again with your linked Google account.

Request deletion without the app

Email Readuo support from the Google email address linked to your account. Use the subject “Readuo account deletion” and say that you want your account and associated data permanently deleted.

Email a deletion request

Contact: plogramer.dev@gmail.com. If your email app does not open, copy this address into your email service. Support will verify account ownership before processing the request. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, or identity documents.

What is deleted?

Your sign-in account, profile and photos, library and uploaded covers, friendships and requests, blocks, Circle posts, reviews, comments and likes, notifications and device tokens, preferences, support requests, and associated reports, moderation and security records are permanently removed from Readuo’s application data. There is no recovery or retention period.

Once processing begins it cannot be cancelled. Temporary processing state is removed when deletion finishes. Content other people have independently saved or downloaded is outside your account and cannot be recalled.
