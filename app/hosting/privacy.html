Privacy at a glance

Readuo uses Google sign-in and Firebase to store your account, library, social content, preferences and uploaded images. Your sign-in email is private authentication data. Your display name and optional profile photo identify you to signed-in readers.

Private shelves are visible only to you. Friends shelves are visible to accepted friends. Public shelves are visible to signed-in Readuo readers, not anonymous website visitors. Circle is friends-only. Only owned books appear in discovery.

Reports and support requests are accessible to authorized moderators. Notification settings control friend requests, accepted requests, likes and comments. Device tokens are used to deliver optional Android push notifications.

The Android app can retain a partial, read-only copy of your own previously loaded library for offline viewing. It does not provide an offline social feed or queue offline edits. Signing out or deleting your account clears the local library cache on that device.

Book searches send your query or ISBN to Open Library, with Google Books used as a fallback. Optional camera/photo access is used only when you choose to scan or attach an image. Upload only images you have the right to share.

Account deletion permanently removes associated Readuo application data, including reports and security records, with no recovery or retention period. Other people’s independent downloads cannot be recalled.
